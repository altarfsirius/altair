Process summary

Capacity, represented party, ownership and authority

These are four different things. Selecting a role does not prove ownership or legal authority; the system only requests evidence and flags its status.

Role = your function in the transaction. Represented party = who you act for. Ownership = title to the asset (needs title evidence). Authority = valid authorization to act (needs a mandate/authority document). “Verified” only records a review outcome; it is not a legal opinion.
